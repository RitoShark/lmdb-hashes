# Code of Conduct

Be reasonable and keep discussions about the work.

## Expected behavior

- Criticize the code or proposal, not the person.
- Assume people are doing their best with the time they have.
- Answer questions constructively, or leave them for someone else.
- Give disagreements room to reach a decision, then respect that decision.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, slurs, threats, or personal attacks.
- Sharing someone's private information without permission.
- Sexual content or unwanted attention.
- Following someone across issues to continue an argument.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces maintained
by this repository. Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or
block repeat or serious offenders.

Report concerns privately to a maintainer through a contact method listed on
their GitHub profile. Include links and enough context to understand what
happened. Reports are handled discreetly and shared only as needed to address
the situation. Do not post another person's private details in a public report.
